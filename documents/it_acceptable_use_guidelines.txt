IT ACCEPTABLE USE GUIDELINES
Document: IT-AUP-2026 | Review Cycle: Annual

1. Purpose
1.1 These guidelines define acceptable use of corporate laptops, cloud accounts, email, and network resources.

2. Permitted and Prohibited Use
2.1 Incidental personal use of company devices is permitted outside core working hours, provided that it does not interfere with job duties, consume excessive bandwidth, or violate Section 2.2.
2.2 Users must not disable endpoint security agents, install unauthorized browser extensions, or share individual API tokens or SSH private keys.
2.3 Users are not entitled to any expectation of privacy on corporate-managed devices or email accounts, except as required by applicable local labor law.

3. Enforcement
3.1 Suspected violations are investigated jointly by IT Security and Human Resources and may lead to access revocation or disciplinary action.
