# Remote Work and Equipment Security Policy

**Policy Reference:** HR-SEC-2026-09  
**Effective Date:** March 1, 2026  
**Applicability:** All Remote, Hybrid, and Traveling Staff  

## Section 1: Work Environment and Home Office Standards

1.1 Remote employees are responsible for maintaining a secure and ergonomically sound workspace. High-speed internet with WPA3 or enterprise WPA2 encryption is required; open public Wi-Fi networks must never be used without an active company VPN connection.

1.2 Screen privacy filters are mandatory whenever working in public spaces, shared co-working offices, or during passenger transit. Employees are not entitled to reimbursement for visual privacy screens purchased outside approved procurement channels.

## Section 2: Hardware Provisioning and Mobile Device Security

2.1 Corporate Laptops: The company provides each full-time employee with one standard-issue corporate laptop. Personal devices (BYOD) are strictly prohibited from accessing production cloud environments, except as permitted under Section 3.2.

2.2 Hardware Maintenance: All corporate equipment must remain enrolled in the company MDM (Mobile Device Management) system. Users must install operating system security patches within seven (7) calendar days of release, unless an emergency zero-day patch window is declared under Section 4.1.

2.3 Lost or Stolen Equipment: Any loss or theft of company equipment must be reported to the IT Helpdesk and Security Operations within two (2) hours of discovery, subject to Clause 4.2.

## Section 3: Expense Reimbursement and Home Office Stipend

3.1 One-Time Setup Stipend: Newly hired remote personnel are eligible for a one-time home office equipment stipend of up to $800 USD, covering monitors, chairs, and peripherals. Receipts must be submitted within sixty (60) days of hire.

3.2 Exceptions and Supplemental Gear: Ergonomic equipment exceeding standard limits requires written pre-approval from the People Operations Director, provided that a physician recommendation is submitted with the expense request.

## Section 4: Incident Reporting and Compliance Audits

4.1 Emergency Security Windows: In the event of an active vulnerability campaign, the CISO may mandate immediate device updates within twenty-four (24) hours, superseding Section 2.2 timelines.

4.2 Loss Escalation: Reports of stolen laptops containing local cache files must include police report documentation within seventy-two (72) hours of the initial notice required under Clause 2.3.
