Enterprise Information Security & Compliance Policy

Policy Code: SEC-COMP-2026 | Classification: Internal | Effective: 2026-02-01

Section 1: Data Classification and Handling

1.1 All company information is classified into four tiers: Public, Internal, Confidential, and Restricted (including PII, PCI-DSS cardholder data, and cryptographic keys).

1.2 Restricted data must never be stored on unencrypted personal devices or transmitted over unapproved consumer messaging applications, except where explicitly authorized in writing by the CISO under Clause 3.2.

Section 2: Access Control and Authentication

2.1 Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) using hardware security keys or phishing-resistant authenticators is mandatory for all production, VPN, and financial systems.

2.2 Principle of Least Privilege: Production database access is granted on a time-bound just-in-time basis (maximum 4 hours) and requires ticket approval. Contractors are not entitled to standing administrative privileges.

Section 3: AI Tooling, Retention, and Exceptions

3.1 Employees must not paste Confidential or Restricted customer data into unvetted third-party public AI services. Only enterprise-approved or local on-premise inference endpoints may process sensitive records.

3.2 Temporary Policy Exceptions: A business unit may request a 90-day exception to Section 1.2 or Section 2.1 by submitting a risk register entry approved by both the CISO and Legal Counsel, provided that compensating controls are documented and reviewed monthly.

Section 4: Regulatory Reporting

4.1 Suspected GDPR or data privacy incidents must be escalated to the Data Protection Officer within four (4) hours of detection so statutory 72-hour supervisory notifications can be evaluated.
